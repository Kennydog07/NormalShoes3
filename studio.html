Make your own collector discs

We master your film, design and print the artwork and packaging, and give you everything you need. Everything is made by Normal Shoes, except that you, or someone you nominate who isn't connected to Normal Shoes and isn't paid to burn, press burn.

How it works

  1. Send your film by share link.
  2. We master it: menus, chapters, colour and sound polish (optional).
  3. We design and print your artwork and blank discs.
  4. You come to a Disc Lab and make and pack your own discs (you, or someone you nominate who isn't connected to Normal Shoes and isn't paid to burn, make the copies).
  5. You leave with your collector set.

How the Disc Lab works

You make the copies of your own film yourself, using our equipment and with our help, or at home with your kit. We charge for mastering, workshops, equipment use and blank printed discs, never for finished copies of a film. Bring only a film you own the rights to, including the music. Copies you make for yourself, or give away free of charge, don't need a BBFC rating. To sell copies, choose a Rated release. You can also nominate someone to make the copies for you, as long as they aren't connected to Normal Shoes and aren't paid to do it.

No burner at home?

That's fine. You can book a Studio Disc Lab and use our equipment, borrow or rent a disc burner from a friend or a local shop, or use the facilities at your university or college if you're a student.
