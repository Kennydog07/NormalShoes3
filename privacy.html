Privacy Policy

How Normal Shoes collects, uses and looks after the personal information you share with us through this website and by email.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is run by Normal Shoes, a small UK business running a filmmaker disc studio and Normal Shoes: the festival of first films. For UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), Normal Shoes is the controller of the personal information described here.

Normal Shoes · Flat 113, Mariner Point, 83 Brighton Road, Shoreham-by-Sea, BN43 6DH · normalshoeseditions@gmail.com

2. What we collect, and why

FormWhat we collectWhyLawful basis
Contact formName, email, topic, your message, and whether you opted in to the newsletter.To reply and, if you ask, to quote for or book a Disc Lab, kit, keepsake or rated release.Legitimate interests; steps at your request before a contract.
NewsletterEmail address.To send news about the Studio, the festival and deadlines.Consent. Unsubscribe at any time.
Festival and film school enquiry formsOrganisation, contact name, email, role, dates or numbers you give, services of interest and your message.To discuss services for your festival or school.Legitimate interests; steps at your request before a contract.

Festival submissions are made through an external submission platform, under that platform's own privacy policy. We receive your entry details from it to run the festival (contract and legitimate interests). Once you buy a service, we keep records of sales because tax law requires us to (legal obligation). Our host automatically records basic technical information (such as IP address and browser) to deliver the site and keep it secure (legitimate interests).

We never sell your information. We only send the newsletter if you've opted in.

3. Who we share it with

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) hosts this website and receives the information you submit through our forms.
  • Google (Gmail) provides our email account.
  • Postal and courier services receive your name and address when we send you an at-home kit.
  • The BBFC receives what's needed to classify a film, if you choose a rated release.

We may also disclose information if the law requires us to.

4. International transfers

Netlify and Google may process information outside the UK. Where that happens, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as the UK–US data bridge or the International Data Transfer Agreement / UK Addendum. Ask us for details.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after our last contact, unless they become a booking.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.
  • Sales records: 6 years from the end of the tax year they relate to.
  • Form submissions held by Netlify: deleted periodically once they've reached our inbox.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. It only stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, which never leaves your device.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your information, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email normalshoeseditions@gmail.com. There's no charge, and we'll reply within one month.

8. Complaints

Please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, helpline 0303 123 1113, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF.

9. Security and changes

The site is served over HTTPS and we use reputable providers. We'll update this page if anything changes. See also our Terms.
