International

Submitting from outside the UK

Normal Shoes: the festival of first films is open to filmmakers worldwide.

Language and subtitles

Films can be in any language, with English subtitles, either burned in or as an .srt file.

Screening files

Digital screening files only: ProRes or high-quality H.264/H.265, and a DCP if you have one. Please don't post anything to us.

Fees

Fees are paid in GBP through the submission platform, for example £XX (GBP). Fee waivers for filmmakers in low-income countries: details TBC.

Deadlines

All deadlines are in UK time (GMT in winter, BST in summer). We write dates in full, for example 14 March 2027.

Rights and ratings

Please confirm you have the rights to screen your film in the UK, including the music. Selected films without a BBFC rating get a local age rating before they're screened.

Travel

We can't cover travel or accommodation, and we can't offer visa support (TBC). If you can't travel, we offer an online Q&A.

If you're selected

You'll get your digital disc file and a printable artwork pack, so you can make your own discs at home. We don't ship kits outside the UK for now (TBC).
