Studio and the festival of first films

Your film. On a disc you made yourself.

Normal Shoes masters your film, designs your artwork and gives you everything you need to make your own collector discs. And twice a year, we premiere first films on the big screen in Worthing.

What we do

The Studio

We master your film, design and print your artwork and blank discs, and you make your own collector discs in a Disc Lab session or at home with a kit.

The Studio and Disc Lab

The festival of first films

Twice a year in Worthing: a spring competition of debut and graduation films, and an autumn edition with Shot on Film and Coast. Open to filmmakers worldwide.

About the festival

For festivals

Laurels, Disc Lab sessions, kits, rated compilations and award-night keepsakes for partner festivals and their filmmakers.

A note from Callum

Photo of Callum to follow

I'm Callum, and I started Normal Shoes after graduating from Brighton Film School. I believe every first film deserves a big screen, and a copy you can hold.

So we do both: we help you make your own collector discs of your film, and twice a year we put first films on the big screen in Worthing.

Callum, founder
