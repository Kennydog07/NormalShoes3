Terms

  1. The film is your first film or your graduation film.
  2. You own the rights to the film, or have permission to use everything in it, including the music.
  3. If your film is selected, we may screen it at the edition, and use stills and up to 30 seconds of clips to promote the festival.
  4. Selected films without a BBFC rating get a local age rating before they're screened.
  5. You keep all rights to your film.
  6. Including your film on the annual compilation is optional, and needs your separate written agreement.
  7. The selection team's decisions are final.
