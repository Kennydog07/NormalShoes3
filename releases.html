Rated releases and the compilation

Once a film is classified by the BBFC, we can make and sell finished discs to anyone.

Rated releases

We handle the BBFC submission, labelling, design and manufacture of finished discs you can sell anywhere. Compilations share one rating across several films.

BBFC classification fees are passed on at cost. As a guide, the 2026 BBFC standard rate is £9.66 per minute, with a minimum fee of £96.60 for works under 10 minutes, plus BBFC handling charges, all excluding VAT. See the BBFC fee tariffs.
