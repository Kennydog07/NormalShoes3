Normal Shoes: the festival of first films

Debut and graduation films on the big screen in Worthing, open to filmmakers worldwide.

Submit your filmWhat's on

What counts as a first film?

A director's debut short, or a graduation or final-year film, completed within the last 2 years.

Do earlier school projects count as my first film?

No. Earlier school projects don't count, so your debut short or graduation film is still eligible.

We co-directed. Can we enter?

Yes, co-directors are fine.

I'm older, or changing career. Am I eligible?

Yes. Older and career-changing filmmakers are very welcome. It's about your first film, not your age.
