For festivals

Services for partner festivals

Help your filmmakers leave with something they can hold, all through the same legal routes we use ourselves.

What we offer

Official Selection laurels

Laurels for your filmmakers' artwork.

Disc Lab sessions and Stations

Sessions or Stations at your events, for your selected filmmakers to make discs of their own films: the filmmaker, or someone they nominate who isn't connected to Normal Shoes and isn't paid to burn, makes the copies (Route A).

At-home kits

Kits carrying your laurels: disc file, blank printed discs, packaging and a guide (Route A).

Rated Best of compilations

BBFC-rated compilations of your programme, which can be sold (Route B).

Award-night keepsakes

Keepsake discs of your award night for the people who took part (Route C).
